# ADR-094: Management Review Candidate vs Action Proposal

Status: Accepted

A management review candidate is only a hypothesis that a position condition needs review. It is not a selected, approved, promoted, or executable action.

Sprint 18.1 introduces proposed management actions as explicit reference-only outputs derived from a management action policy. Proposed actions are allowed to be `exit_position` or `reduce_position`, but they remain non-executable and cannot become top-level final actions.
